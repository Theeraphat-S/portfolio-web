# 0002. Palette Migration to Flutter Cyan & Electric Blue

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The portfolio previously utilized an Emerald/Mint green accent palette (#10b981, #34d399). While high-contrast, the user specializes primarily as a **Mobile Application Developer in Flutter & Dart**. Flutter's core visual identity is centered on vibrant Cyan and Electric Blue tones. To align the portfolio's branding with the engineer's core technical specialty, a deliberate palette migration was proposed and evaluated.

## Decision

1. **Brand Identity Palette (Dual Gradient)**:
   - Primary accents, action buttons, interactive hover borders, particle sparks, and the custom cursor transition from Emerald to a **Cyan (#06b6d4, #38bdf8) to Electric Blue (#0284c7, #2563eb)** gradient system.
   - The brutalist editorial typography (.text-shiny) and background sheen beams now sweep across Sky Blue, White, and Deep Cyan.
2. **Selective Indicator Retention (UX Telemetry)**:
   - Standard live operational telemetry—specifically the **"Available for Opportunity"** pulsing beacon, device battery/status dot, and real-time system pulse—retains standard **Emerald Green (#10b981)** to maintain universal recognition of active/online health status.
3. **Interactive 3D Device Showcase**:
   - The device backdrop glow orb, phone shell reflection, and feature actions align with Cyan & Blue, matching production Flutter app branding.

## Consequences

- Direct alignment with the Flutter & Dart brand ecosystem, immediately communicating the developer's core specialty to recruiters.
- Enhanced contrast and visual depth across both Dark and Light themes.
- Clear semantic separation between brand identity (Cyan/Blue) and real-time operational status (Green pulse).
